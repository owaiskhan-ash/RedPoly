01 / DELIVERY

SHIPPING

The storefront estimates Standard delivery in 4 days and Express delivery in 2 days for valid Indian PIN codes. These are demo estimates only: the PIN lookup identifies a location, but it is not connected to a courier service and does not confirm serviceability or an arrival date.

The demo checkout offers Standard shipping (estimated 4 days) at ₹30 or Express shipping (estimated 2 days) at ₹120. Standard shipping is free when the item subtotal reaches ₹2,000, and Express is also free from ₹5,000. Orders with an item subtotal of ₹3,500 or more also include a free gift. These are storefront demo rates; no courier service or actual delivery charge is connected.

02 / RETURNS

RETURNS

Return requests must be made within 7 days. Items must be unused and in their original packaging. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method.

03 / CHANGE OF PLANS

CANCELLATION

For a real order, you may request cancellation before it is dispatched by contacting support. Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation is not available; the returns policy applies where eligible.

The current demo does not create real orders, so there is nothing to cancel.
