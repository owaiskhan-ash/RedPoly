01 / DELIVERY

SHIPPING

The storefront estimates Standard delivery in 4 days and Express delivery in 2 days for valid Indian PIN codes. These are demo estimates only: the PIN lookup identifies a location, but it is not connected to a courier service and does not confirm serviceability or an arrival date.

The demo checkout offers Standard shipping (estimated 4 days) at ₹30 or Express shipping (estimated 2 days) at ₹120. Standard shipping is free when the item subtotal reaches ₹1,499, and Express is also free from ₹5,000. These are storefront demo rates; no courier service or actual delivery charge is connected.

02 / RETURNS

RETURNS

Return requests must be made within 7 days. Items must be unused and in their original packaging. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method.

03 / CHANGE OF PLANS

CANCELLATION

For a real order, you may request cancellation before it is dispatched by contacting support. Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation is not available; the returns policy applies where eligible.

The current demo does not create real orders, so there is nothing to cancel.

04 / YOUR DATA

PRIVACY

This storefront checkout is a demo. It does not send or save the name, address, email, mobile number, or card values entered in the demo form. No payment is processed and no real order is created.

The shopping bag is stored in this browser's local storage. A temporary demo order summary is stored in session storage. When you use PIN lookup, the PIN code is sent to a public postal lookup service to retrieve city and state. The newsletter form currently shows a demo confirmation only; it does not submit or save your email.

Google Fonts are loaded from Google when pages use those typefaces. A complete privacy notice should be reviewed and published before real customer data is collected.

05 / SITE NOTES

REDPOLY character names, designs, and artwork are reserved. See the character IP notice. Full terms for real sales should be published before paid ordering is enabled.
