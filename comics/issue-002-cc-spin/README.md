# Issue 02: CC Does a Spin

Six-page issue. The stunt crew meets CC-01, a white four-wheel-drive car from the future, and the night turns into a spin, a chase and a jump over a broken bridge.

- `pages/page-01.webp` to `pages/page-06.webp` are the finished art at 2048 x 2048.
- `cover.webp` is the shelf preview, made from page 02.
- `issue.json` is the reader manifest. Each page also carries a `shop` list of the products that appear in that scene, which the reader shows as small linked chips under the page.
- The issue label baked into the bottom margin of the supplied art read "ISSUE 01", so that strip was replaced with the surrounding cream before export; the reader supplies the correct issue and page numbers.
