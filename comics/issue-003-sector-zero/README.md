# Issue 03: Sector Zero

Six pages, part one. The restricted future blasters that Poko and Reno fired in issue 02 tripped a silent beacon: a hatch opens under the turbo track and something huge climbs out of Sector Zero.

- `pages/page-01.webp` to `pages/page-06.webp` are the finished art at 2048 x 2048.
- `cover.webp` is the shelf preview, made from the final panel of page 06 (the Mecha Rover helmet reveal).
- `issue.json` is the reader manifest. Each page carries a `shop` list of the products seen in that scene, which the reader shows as small linked chips under the page.
- The label baked into the bottom margin of the supplied art read "ISSUE 02 • PAGE 01" to "PAGE 06", so those rows were replaced with a clean cream band measured from the margin above them, matching the issue 02 files; the reader supplies the correct issue and page numbers.

## Part 2

Mecha Rover shifts into overdrive, and the chase takes the crew to the Sector Zero Magnet Core.

- `part-2/pages/` holds the six finished pages at 2048 × 2048. The original Issue 03 page labels are intact.
- `part-2/cover.webp` is cropped from Mecha Rover's transformation in the first panel of page 05.
- `part-2/issue.json` is the separate reader manifest. Part 1 retains its original catalog ID so existing links still open the same pages.
