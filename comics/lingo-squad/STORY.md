# CLICKBAIT — Issue 04 story pitch

**Working title:** CLICKBAIT
**Series:** Sector Zero Files · Lingo Squad
**Format:** 6 pages × 4 panels (24 panels), 2048 × 2048 webp pages, same reader manifest pattern as Issues 02–03
**Cast:** Lingo, Rino, Tino, Gino, the Magnetic Blocks (inanimate)
**Logline:** Lingo tries to teach the crew about magnets, the crew tries to build a bridge, and the bridge has other plans.

---

## Why this story is built this way

The products hand us three comedy machines, so the story is engineered around them instead of pretending they don't exist:

1. **The dinos have no arms and no steering.** They are press-and-go toys. Rino can only move if somebody presses him. That single fact is the funniest thing in the cast — a character whose whole personality is "send it" who physically cannot start himself.
2. **Lingo explains everything, including things nobody asked.** His teaching reflex should interrupt danger at the worst possible moment, and his vocabulary asides should land as punchlines rather than lessons.
3. **The blocks are inanimate.** They never talk, never move on their own, and never get faces. Every joke about them is a physics joke, and Tino's terror of ordinary objects is the release valve.

The emotional spine is small and warm: four toys who cannot do much alone, getting one thing done together — badly, loudly, and just in time.

## Page-by-page script

### PAGE 1 — "THE CRATE"

**Panel 1.** Wide. A Sector Zero tunnel at night. RINO lies flat on his belly, wheels spinning in the air like a beetle.
- CAPTION: DEEP IN SECTOR ZERO…
- RINO: PRESS ME. PRESS ME. PRESS ME.
- GINO: You are a press-and-go dinosaur. You cannot press yourself. That is the "go".
- TINO (small): What if nobody ever presses us again?

**Panel 2.** LINGO waddles in, screen bright, flashcards fanned out of his gear clamp.
- LINGO: Namaste, doston! Today's lesson: MAGNETISM. Noun. The reason your plans stick together — briefly.

**Panel 3.** A crate. Stencilled: MAGNETIC BLOCKS · 64 PCS · DO NOT STACK. Cyan light leaks out of the seams.
- RINO: Ooh. Forbidden Lego.
- LINGO: Sixty-four pieces. Sixty-four ways this goes wrong. I have numbered them.
- GINO: He has a spreadsheet.
- LINGO: It's laminated.

**Panel 4.** TINO's face very close to the crate, one eye huge.
- TINO: They're not moving. Nothing is moving them. WHAT IF THEY'RE WAITING?
- CAPTION (small): The blocks said nothing. They never do.

### PAGE 2 — "THE LESSON"

**Panel 1.** LINGO holds up two rods in his clamps.
- LINGO: Opposites attract! Like poles repel! Say it with me!
- GINO: Like poles repel. Like Tino and fun.
- TINO: I have fun. I fun very carefully.

**Panel 2.** GINO presses RINO's back. RINO rockets head-first into the crate. Blocks explode into the air.
- SFX: CLACK CLACK CLACK
- RINO: YOLOOOO—

**Panel 3.** RINO, still, covered in blocks like a chandelier, grinning.
- LINGO: Congratulations. You are now forty percent refrigerator.
- RINO: I'm MAGNETIC.
- GINO: You're DECORATIVE.

**Panel 4.** LINGO tugs a block off RINO — his clamp snaps onto it and he is now also wearing blocks.
- TINO: Great. Two fridges. One crisis.
- CAPTION: The blocks remained inanimate. Somehow that was the scary part.

### PAGE 3 — "THE GAP"

**Panel 1.** Wide: a chasm splits the tunnel. Across it, the Magnet Core hums.
- GINO: Correction. The gap is 3.4 metres. Our legs: zero. Rino's plan: also zero.
- RINO: My plan is called "AAAAAA".

**Panel 2.** TINO unrolls a scroll so long it leaves the panel.
- TINO: Step one: worry. Step two: worry with a diagram. Step 1,847: bridge.
- GINO: Page 900 is just the word "no".
- TINO: In a nice font.

**Panel 3.** LINGO, delighted, one clamp raised like a lecturer.
- LINGO: Vocabulary break! JUGAAD. Noun. Engineering, but with vibes.
- GINO: That is not in the dictionary.
- LINGO: It is in MY dictionary. Page 12. Laminated.

**Panel 4.** RINO braced at the edge, wheels buzzing. He does not move.
- RINO (tiny): ...someone press me.
- TINO: That is the most terrifying sentence I have ever heard.

### PAGE 4 — "BUILD"

**Panel 1.** GINO counting blocks into rows. TINO chewing nervously — there is a ball in his mouth.
- GINO: Sixty-three. We're missing one.
- TINO: IT'S BEHIND US. IT'S ALWAYS BEHIND US.
- GINO: It's in your mouth.
- TINO: ...I've been holding that for an hour.

**Panel 2.** Build montage. TINO places a block with unbearable care; LINGO points at a triangle.
- LINGO: TRIANGLE. The strongest shape, and the sassiest.
- GINO: Forty degrees. Not forty-one. I can hear forty-one.

**Panel 3.** RINO headbutts a loose pile. It snaps into a perfect bridge with one last CLACK.
- GINO: That shouldn't work.
- RINO: And yet.

**Panel 4.** LINGO steps onto the bridge to demonstrate load-bearing. Every block in the tunnel flies to his body. He is now the middle of the bridge.
- LINGO (muffled): Structurally, I am load-bearing.
- TINO: He's the bridge now.
- GINO: He's the bridge now.

### PAGE 5 — "THE CORE"

**Panel 1.** The Magnet Core wakes: rings of blue light, everything in the room leaning toward it. The crew slides.
- SFX: WHUMMM
- GINO: Also, the floor is now the ceiling.

**Panel 2.** LINGO stretched between the bridge and the core, holding on with both gear clamps.
- LINGO: I am experiencing STRONG ATTRACTION and I do not consent!
- RINO: You're being repulsive!
- LINGO: I'm being POLARIZING.

**Panel 3.** GINO, calm, pointing.
- GINO: Cut the power. It's a magnet, not a monster. Magnets do not chase you.
- (The core turns. It chases them.)
- GINO: ...Correction.

**Panel 4.** TINO alone in front of the core, tiny.
- TINO: Are you... alive?
- (Silence. One block clicks somewhere in the dark.)
- CAPTION: The core said nothing either. It was a very quiet apocalypse.

### PAGE 6 — "STICK WITH ME"

**Panel 1.** LINGO, fully block-armoured, walks across the gap as a living span. The dinos roll over his back.
- RINO: You're the best bridge I've ever met.
- LINGO: Low bar. I'm the only bridge you've met.

**Panel 2.** Everyone safe on the far side. LINGO still stuck in the middle of the gap.
- GINO: We'll come back for you.
- LINGO: That's what they said about the warranty.

**Panel 3.** The core powers down. Blocks rain off LINGO, who ends flat on the floor like a dropped slice of bread.
- LINGO: Fun fact: that is what we call a MAGNETIC personality.
- TINO: What if we're the inanimate ones?
- GINO: Go to sleep, Tino.

**Panel 4.** The crew rolls off into the dark. Foreground: one last wobbly block tower, leaning.
- CAPTION: NEXT ISSUE: LINGO LEARNS THE WORD "GRAVITY".
- LINGO (off-panel): GRAVITY. Noun. The reason—
- SFX: THUD

## Joke bank (spares and punch-ups)

**Magnet puns**
- "I find this very attractive." / "You're a fridge."
- "We're having a polarizing debate."
- "Stick with me." — "That's the plan. Literally."
- "This is riveting." (nobody knows what a rivet is; Gino explains for half a page)
- "Opposites attract. That's why Tino and I work." — "We don't work." — "Opposites."
- "You can't push my buttons." — Lingo's buttons get pushed.

**Hindi / Hinglish**
- "Chipak gaya." (It stuck.) — said with total defeat.
- "Bas." (Enough.) — Gino's entire vocabulary during the crisis.
- "Shabd." (Word.) — Lingo's safe word, which nobody respects.
- "Jugaad" — engineering, but with vibes.

**Running gags**
- The blocks never speak; every page ends on their silence.
- Rino asks to be pressed at least once per page. Somebody always presses him at the worst moment.
- Tino's mouth is always holding one piece of the set.
- Lingo defines a word one beat before it happens to them.
- Gino says "correction" after his own confident statement.

## Two alternate pitches (if CLICKBAIT isn't the one)

**A. "PRESS & GO"** — A Sector Zero race where nobody can start without help. Rino sabotages himself, Tino plans a route with 400 contingencies, Gino times everyone to the millisecond, and Lingo commentates the whole thing with vocabulary cards. Ends with all three dinos finishing as one rolling pile. Pure gag machine, no villain needed.

**B. "THE MISSING SHABD"** — One card goes missing from Lingo's 100, and he becomes unbearable: he cannot name the thing he's looking for, so he describes it, at length, while the crew is chased by a very patient Mecha Rover. The card is in Tino's mouth. It always is. Warm, wordy, and the most "Lingo" of the three.
