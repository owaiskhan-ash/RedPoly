02 / BROWSER STORAGE

WHAT WE STORE IN YOUR BROWSER

When you browse as a guest (or while syncing alongside an account), the storefront saves lightweight state directly inside your browser's localStorage and sessionStorage so your cart and page preferences survive reloads without requiring tracking cookies.

Storage Key Type Purpose
redpoly-cart-v1 localStorage Saves your selected toys, validated against the official REDPOLY catalog and quantities.
redpoly-wishlist-v1 localStorage Remembers toys you hearted in your Saved list so they stay pinned across page visits.
redpoly-guest-address-v1 localStorage Optionally saves a delivery address draft locally if you save your address on My Account without signing in with Google.
redpoly-shipping-method-v1 sessionStorage Remembers whether Standard or Express demo shipping is selected during checkout.
redpoly-demo-order-v1 sessionStorage Holds your temporary demo receipt summary for display on the Thank You screen.
redpoly-splash-loads sessionStorage Tracks intro splash frequency within the current tab so the intro animation only plays periodically.

You can clear all locally stored items at any time from your browser's site data settings, or by removing items inside the Cart and Saved drawers.

03 / GOOGLE SIGN-IN & CLOUD SYNC

OPTIONAL ACCOUNT & DELIVERY PROFILE DATA

REDPOLY offers an optional Sign in with Google feature powered by Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. You never need to sign in to browse toys, read comics, or test guest checkout.

WHEN YOU SIGN IN WITH GOOGLE

  • Account Profile: Firebase receives your unique user ID (uid), verified email address, display name, and Google profile photo URL (only HTTPS image links are stored).
  • Cart & Wishlist Sync: Your guest cart and wishlist merge with your signed-in account so your selections stay consistent across devices.
  • Private Delivery Details: When you place a demo order while signed in with the "Save delivery details" option enabled, your full name, email, 10-digit mobile number, street address, 6-digit PIN code, city, and state are stored in an isolated private record (/users/{userId}/private/profile) so checkout can autofill them next time.
  • Demo Order History: Signed-in demo orders (order reference, item breakdown, INR subtotal/GST/shipping totals, and shipping address snapshot) are saved under your account so you can review them in the Account drawer.
Owner-only access isolation: Cloud Firestore security rules verify your authenticated Google identity and require a verified email before allowing reads or writes. Other visitors cannot view your profile, cart, wishlist, or order history, and listing other users is blocked at the database rule level.

04 / CHECKOUT & PAYMENT FIELDS

HOW DEMO CHECKOUT HANDLES YOUR INPUTS

The checkout page operates strictly in demo preview mode:

  • No Real Card Data: The payment section displays read-only placeholder fields (REDPOLY DEMO, 4242 4242 4242 4242). We do not accept, transmit, tokenize, or store credit card, debit card, UPI, or bank credentials.
  • Guest Checkout Is Local Only: If you submit the demo checkout form without signing in with Google, your name, email, mobile number, and street address are used only for client-side validation and are never sent to our servers or saved to a database. Only your non-PII demo summary (order number, items, totals, PIN code) is placed temporarily in sessionStorage for the Thank You page.
  • Signed-In Checkout Is Optional: If you are signed in with Google at checkout, your demo order receipt is saved to your Firestore account history and, unless you uncheck the save option, your delivery details are stored in your private profile for future autofill.

05 / THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

EXTERNAL REQUESTS MADE BY THE SITE

To deliver typography, authentication, and location autofill, your browser communicates with the following third-party services when those features are used:

  • Google Firebase (Auth & Cloud Firestore): Handles optional Google Sign-In sessions and cloud persistence for signed-in users, subject to Google's privacy terms.
  • India Postal PIN Code Lookup (api.postalpincode.in): When you enter a 6-digit Indian PIN code in the checkout form, only that 6-digit PIN code is sent to look up the corresponding district/city and state. No name, phone number, or street address is included in the lookup request.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com): Serves display and body fonts across the storefront, which transmits standard browser request headers (such as IP address) to Google's font CDN.

REDPOLY does not embed third-party ad networks, behavioral retargeting pixels, or cross-site advertising trackers.

JOIN THE REDPOLY CREW NEWSLETTER

The Join the REDPOLY Crew email form on the home page currently operates as a static UI preview. Submitting an email address triggers an immediate on-screen confirmation toast and resets the form in your browser without transmitting, subscribing, or storing your email address on a mailing list.

If live newsletter or drop alerts are introduced later, this Privacy Policy will be updated with clear opt-in and unsubscribe details before any email list collection begins.

07 / SECURITY & RETENTION

HOW DATA IS PROTECTED & KEPT

We apply strict input validation, owner-scoped Firestore rules, immutable order receipt checks, and standard HTTP security headers (X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff, Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin, and restrictive Permissions-Policy).

  • Local browser data stays in your browser until you clear site storage or remove items (and sessionStorage clears when your browser session ends).
  • Signed-in cloud records remain tied to your account so your cart, wishlist, and saved demo orders stay accessible when you return.
  • No server logs of guest card info: Because no real payment processor is connected, sensitive payment credentials never touch our storefront or database.
Important reminder: Please do not enter sensitive financial credentials or confidential information into any demo form. Before commercial launch, please review active contact channels and deletion workflows with legal counsel.

08 / YOUR CHOICES & CHILDREN

CONTROLLING YOUR DATA & FAMILY PRIVACY

Your Choices: You can use the entire storefront without signing in, uncheck delivery profile saving during signed-in checkout, sign out from the header account menu at any time, or wipe local cart and saved lists directly from your browser settings.

Children's Privacy: Although REDPOLY creates toys and comics enjoyed by families and collectors (with specific products rated 3+ or 14+), online accounts and checkout forms are intended for adults, parents, or legal guardians. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children without parental supervision.

Policy Changes: As REDPOLY transitions from demo mode to full order fulfillment or adds new features, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and publish revised terms before collecting new categories of customer data.
