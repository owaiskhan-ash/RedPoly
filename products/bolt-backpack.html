DELIVERY + RETURNS

How do the Blue and Red variants differ?

Both are the same ₹899 everyday BOLT backpack with padded straps, front buckle straps, and a detachable side mini pouch. Blue features the sky-blue & cream "Good 4You" print with the "Don't Look Back" pouch; Red features the maroon & cream "Born Right" design with the "Bee You" pouch.

Is the side mini pouch included?

Yes. Every Bolt Backpack comes with its matching detachable zippered side pouch clipped to the side webbing loop.

How are shipping and returns handled?

Standard delivery takes 4 days (₹30) and Express takes 2 days (₹120). Both Standard and Express shipping are free on orders of ₹2,000 or more. Orders of ₹3,500 or more include a free gift. Returns are accepted within 7 days if unused and in the original packaging.
