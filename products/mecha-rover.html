HOW IT WORKSREDPOLY / TOYS

BUILD IT, THEN BUILD IT AGAIN

ONE SET.
TWO MACHINES.

Start with the red truck cab, bolt on the wheels and clip the grey water cannon and launcher to the roof. That is the fire rescue truck. Pull it apart with the screwdriver and the same pieces stack up into a standing robot with a blue helmet, a blue lower body and tyres on the shoulders.

Every piece is chunky, rounded plastic made for small hands, so the screws are big, the joins are forgiving and nothing needs batteries. The set is marked for ages three and up, and a grown up should be nearby for the first build.
