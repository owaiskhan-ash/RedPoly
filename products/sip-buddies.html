DELIVERY + RETURNS

How do the four designs differ?

Only in looks. The cow, cat and puppy have a cream body with a matching collar and cord. The apple has a bare polished steel body with a red apple lid. All four hold 380 ML and cost ₹899.

Is the small figure in the photos included?

No. Some photos show a small matching figure next to the bottle. It is there for the picture only. You get one bottle with its character lid, straw-spout cap and carry cord.

Can it hold hot drinks?

The bottle is insulated, so it can carry warm or cold drinks. Let very hot drinks cool to a safe temperature first, and do not let a child drink a hot drink through the straw. Wash it by hand before first use and after each use.

How are shipping and returns handled?

Standard delivery takes 4 days (₹30) and Express takes 2 days (₹120). Both Standard and Express shipping are free on orders of ₹2,000 or more. Orders of ₹3,500 or more include a free gift. Returns are accepted within 7 days if unused and in the original packaging.
