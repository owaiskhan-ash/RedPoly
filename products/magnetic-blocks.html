DELIVERY + RETURNS

What is the difference between the 25, 36, 42 and 64 PCS packs?

Every pack uses the same colourful straight sticks, curved arches and magnetic spheres — the larger packs include more bars and balls so kids can build bigger towers, windmills, domes and multi-structure models at once, or connect multiple packs together.

Are Magnetic Blocks safe for young builders?

Yes, the box is rated for ages 3 and up. Pieces are sized for small hands with smooth, rounded plastic edges and enclosed magnets, and need no batteries or cords.

How is shipping charged?

Standard delivery takes 4 days and costs ₹30. It is free on orders of ₹2,000 or more. Express takes 2 days and costs ₹120, or nothing from ₹5,000. Orders of ₹3,500 or more also get a free gift. All prices on the site include GST.

What is the return policy?

Return requests must be made within 7 days. Items must be unused and in their original packaging. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method.
