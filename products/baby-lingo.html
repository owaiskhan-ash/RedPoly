How does Baby Lingo work?

Each of the 100 double-sided flashcards carries a black barcode strip at the bottom. Scan the strip on the robot and Baby Lingo speaks the card aloud in English or Hindi — the word, the rhyme or the story — with matching reactions on the face screen.

What comes inside the Baby Lingo box?

You get the Baby Lingo learning robot and 100 double-sided educational flashcards — words, rhymes, stories and picture themes in English and Hindi. You will need 2 batteries, which are not included.

What age is Baby Lingo for?

The box recommends 6 months and up. It works as a first-words companion from babyhood right through the toddler years, during tummy time, story time and screen-free wind-down.

How are shipping and returns handled?

Standard delivery takes 4 days (₹30) and Express takes 2 days (₹120). Both Standard and Express shipping are free on orders of ₹2,000 or more. Orders of ₹3,500 or more include a free gift. Returns are accepted within 7 days if unused and in the original box.
