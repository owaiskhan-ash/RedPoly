DELIVERY + RETURNS

How does the water mist exhaust spray work?

Open the small rubber cap on top of the rear deck behind the cockpit, fill with clean tap water using the supplied dropper, and press the Spray button on the remote. Built-in LEDs illuminate the mist as a glowing blue turbo trail.

What batteries come in the box and what do I need?

The car comes with a rechargeable 3.7V 1600mAh battery and USB charging cable in the box. Only the 2.4 GHz handheld gamepad needs 2 × 1.5V AA batteries (sold separately).

How are shipping and returns handled?

Standard delivery takes 4 days (₹30, free from ₹2,000) and Express takes 2 days (₹120, free from ₹5,000). Orders of ₹3,500 or more include a free gift. Returns are accepted within 7 days if unused and in the original box.
