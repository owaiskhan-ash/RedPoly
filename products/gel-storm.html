Soak the water beads, tip them into the see-through hopper, and charge the blaster over USB. One switch takes you between automatic and manual fire, and the drum magazine underneath holds the next load.

It is about 58 cm from stock to suppressor, with a stock that adjusts, a grip shaped for a comfortable hold and clip-on accessories you can move or take off. Three play modes live in the same body: gel beads, soft shells and a water spray, so one blaster covers three different games.
